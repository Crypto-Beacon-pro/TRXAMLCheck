Everything you need to know about TRX AML verification, USDT TRC20 compliance, TRON risk scoring, sanctions screening, and the TRX AML Check platform.

What is TRX AML Check?

TRX AML Check is the most advanced TRON blockchain AML (Anti-Money Laundering) compliance verification platform. It instantly screens any TRX wallet, USDT TRC20 address, or TRC20/TRC10 token holder for AML risk, sanctions exposure, mixer usage, and on-chain forensic indicators — providing signed compliance reports from just $0.10 USDT per scan.

How does TRX AML Check scan a TRON wallet?

TRX AML Check retrieves the complete on-chain transaction history of the target TRX address from the TRON blockchain, traces fund flows across up to 10 hops through the TRON transaction graph, cross-references all counterparties against 15+ global sanctions lists including OFAC, EU, and UN, detects TRON mixer and obfuscation patterns, and computes a composite AML risk score from 0 (clean) to 100 (critical risk) using 400+ TRON-specific indicators.

What TRON tokens does TRX AML Check support?

TRX AML Check supports TRX (native TRON coin), USDT TRC20 (Tether on TRON), USDC TRC20, BTT (BitTorrent Token), SUN, JST (JUST), WIN (WINkLink), and all TRC10 and TRC20 standard tokens on the TRON mainnet. Smart contract address analysis and TRC20 token holder compliance checks are fully supported.

Why is AML compliance critical for USDT TRC20?

USDT TRC20 is one of the highest-volume stablecoins used globally, including in high-risk jurisdictions. Because TRC20 transfers are fast and low-cost, they are frequently used in layering stages of money laundering. Exchanges, payment processors, and OTC desks handling USDT TRC20 have strict AML obligations — checking sender and recipient TRX addresses before settling large transfers protects against sanctions exposure and regulatory liability.

How is TRX AML Check different from Tronscan?

Tronscan is a TRON block explorer that shows raw transaction data. TRX AML Check goes far deeper — it performs automated AML risk analysis, multi-hop fund flow tracing, sanctions screening against 15+ global lists, mixer and dark market detection, and generates structured compliance reports (PDF, JSON, CSV) with risk scores. TRX AML Check is a compliance tool, while Tronscan is a data explorer.

TRX AML Check screens against 15+ global sanctions databases updated in real time: OFAC SDN List, OFAC Consolidated Non-SDN Lists, EU Consolidated Sanctions, UN Security Council Sanctions, UK HM Treasury Financial Sanctions, FATF High-Risk Jurisdictions, Swiss SECO Sanctions, Australian DFAT Sanctions, plus crypto-specific intelligence databases covering TRON-linked sanctioned entities and illicit TRON service providers.

What is the difference between direct and indirect TRON sanctions exposure?

Direct TRON sanctions exposure means the checked TRX address appears directly on an OFAC, EU, or UN sanctions list. Indirect exposure means the TRX wallet has transacted with a sanctioned entity within the analyzed TRON graph (within 10 hops). The TRX AML report clearly indicates hop distance, transaction amount direction, and whether the wallet sent to or received from the sanctioned TRON counterparty — enabling proportional compliance decisions.

What is the TRX AML risk score scale?

The TRX AML risk score runs from 0 to 100. Score 0–25 indicates a clean or very low-risk TRON wallet with no detected exposure to illicit activity. Score 26–50 indicates moderate risk — some indirect exposure or elevated counterparty risk warranting enhanced due diligence. Score 51–75 indicates high risk — significant exposure to sanctions, mixers, or dark markets requiring immediate compliance action. Score 76–100 is critical risk — direct sanctions match or confirmed illicit TRON activity, requiring transaction blocking and SAR filing.
